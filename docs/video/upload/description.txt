I built an AI technical interviewer that runs entirely on a Mac: it joins a Google Meet, tells the candidate it is an AI,
asks questions drawn from the resume and the job description, listens, follows up, answers the candidate's own questions,
and writes an advisory report. Local models only (Ollama, whisper.cpp, Piper). A person makes the hiring decision.
Code and docs: https://github.com/tuwallace/senjin

HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS
The latency figures (about 2 seconds to first sound, 19 turns) come from one live interview with one human candidate on one
Mac. The fairness and repeatability checks use a small set of paired transcripts. They are checks, not benchmarks.
